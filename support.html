Restoring your purchase

If you bought Full Access and the app shows the free version, open the app → Settings → Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy. Family Sharing members can restore the same way.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. To request a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Speak Mode isn't hearing me

Check that the app has microphone and speech recognition permission in iOS Settings → Citizen Ready. Speech recognition runs on your device and nothing is recorded. You can always type your answer instead.

Which test will I take?

If you filed Form N-400 before October 20, 2025, you take the 2008 test (100 questions). If you filed on or after that date, you take the 2025 test (128 questions). You can change this any time in Settings → Test Track.

Answers that change

Some answers depend on current officeholders or your state. Always confirm at uscis.gov/citizenship/testupdates before your interview.
