Citizen Ready: Thai

Privacy Policy — Citizen Ready: Thai

Last updated: August 6, 2026

The short version

Citizen Ready does not collect your data. There is no account, no sign-up, and no analytics. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

We say this plainly because we mean it — not as marketing language, but because the app is built so that collecting your data is not technically possible.

What we do not collect

We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or postal address
  • Your immigration status, case number, Form N-400 details, or interview date
  • Your voice recordings or speech
  • Your study progress, quiz scores, or readiness score
  • Your location
  • Advertising identifiers or device fingerprints
  • Contacts, photos, calendar, or any other device data

There is no user account because the app does not need one. There is no analytics SDK. There are no advertisements and no advertising partners.

What stays on your device

The following is stored locally on your iPhone and never leaves it:

  • Your study progress and question history
  • Your readiness score and streak
  • Your settings, including language preference and test track
  • The officeholder answers you look up and save (for example, your senators or governor)
  • Your interview date, if you choose to enter one

This information is stored in your device's app storage. If you delete the app, it is deleted with it. We have no copy and no way to retrieve it.

Microphone and speech recognition

Citizen Ready lets you practice saying answers aloud, because the real USCIS civics test is spoken.

  • The microphone is used only while you are actively practicing a spoken answer.
  • Speech recognition runs on your device, using Apple's on-device Speech framework. Your voice is not sent to us or to any server.
  • Your voice is not recorded, saved, or stored. Audio is processed in real time and discarded immediately.
  • If on-device speech recognition is unavailable on your device, the app switches you to multiple choice rather than sending audio elsewhere.

You may deny or revoke microphone access at any time in iOS Settings. The app remains fully usable without it.

Network access

Citizen Ready works entirely offline. All civics questions, translations, and audio are included in the app.

The app connects to the internet only when you:

  • Tap a link to an official website, such as uscis.gov, senate.gov, or house.gov, to look up a current officeholder. These open in Safari and are governed by those sites' own privacy policies.
  • Make or restore a purchase, which is handled entirely by Apple.

We operate no servers and receive no data from the app.

Purchases

The optional one-time purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment information, credit card number, or Apple ID.

Apple provides us only with anonymous, aggregate sales information. Apple's handling of your purchase is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

The purchase supports Family Sharing, which is managed entirely by Apple.

Children

Citizen Ready is intended for adults preparing for United States naturalization. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Third parties

Citizen Ready contains no third-party SDKs, no analytics libraries, no advertising networks, and no tracking of any kind.

Civics questions and answers are taken from public-domain materials published by U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS). Citizen Ready is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by USCIS or the United States government.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold any of your information, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, export, or delete on your behalf.

You control all of your data directly:

  • View it — it is all visible in the app
  • Delete some of it — use Settings → Reset Progress
  • Delete all of it — delete the app from your device

This satisfies our obligations under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and similar laws, because we are not a data controller or processor of any personal information.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and note the change in the app's release notes. If we ever begin collecting data — which we have no plans to do — we will say so clearly and ask for your consent first.
